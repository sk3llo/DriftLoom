The first DriftLoom release: a free YouTube and SoundCloud client for iPhone, iPad and Android, with no ads,
no tracking and no account.

## Install

- **iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later):** download `DriftLoom-1.0.ipa` and install it with SideStore, AltStore or
  Sideloadly. Follow the [install guide](https://github.com/sk3llo/DriftLoom/blob/main/docs/install-ios.md). With a
  free Apple ID the app has to be refreshed every 7 days.
- **Android (6.0 or later):** download `DriftLoom-1.0.apk` and open it. DriftLoom installs alongside NewPipe and
  doesn't replace it.

Check a download against its `.sha256` file before installing.

## What's in it

- Search YouTube and SoundCloud with filters; channels, playlists, Mixes, trending, live, music charts and podcasts.
- Subscriptions without an account, with groups, a combined **What's new** feed and optional notifications.
- Background audio, Picture in Picture, audio-only mode, chapters, subtitles, speed and resolution limits.
- Queues, autoplay, history, saved videos and local playlists that stay on your device.
- Downloads for offline playback, Wi-Fi-only by default.
- Import subscriptions from NewPipe backups or Google Takeout; back up and restore your library.
- Opens YouTube and SoundCloud links from the share sheet.
- On iOS: English, Ukrainian and Portuguese, and a built-in user guide (**Settings → Help → User guide**).

The Android app is based on NewPipe 0.29.1. The iOS app is a new native app.

## Known limitations

- YouTube sometimes asks networks to "confirm you're not a bot". DriftLoom tells you when that happens and doesn't
  try to get around it; see [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/sk3llo/DriftLoom/blob/main/docs/user-guide/troubleshooting.md).
- The iPad layout has only been tested on the simulator.

## Reporting problems

Use **Report** on an error screen to open a prefilled GitHub issue. On iOS, **Settings → Help → Report a problem**
does the same; on Android, **About & FAQ → Questions and problems** opens the issue tracker. Nothing is sent until
you submit it.
