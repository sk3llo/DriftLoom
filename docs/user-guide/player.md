# The player

Tap a video to open the player. The video is at the top; below it are the title, the channel, buttons to **Save**, **Share**, add to the **Queue** or a **Playlist** and **Download**, and a switch between **Related**, **Comments**, **Description** and **Queue**.

![The player with its controls showing](images/player.jpg)

## Controls on the video

Tap the video to show or hide the controls. They hide by themselves after a few seconds of playback.

- **Middle**: back 10 seconds, play/pause, forward 10 seconds.
- **Bottom**: the time played, the **seek bar**, the video's length, and **full screen**. Live streams show *Live* instead of a length.
- **Top**: playback **speed**, **resize**, **audio only**, **AirPlay** and **Picture in Picture**. See [Speed, quality, subtitles and more](player-options.md).

## Seeking and chapters

Drag along the seek bar. While you drag, a preview frame shows where you will land, with the chapter's name and the time.

![Scrubbing with a preview](images/player-scrub.jpg)

When a video's description lists chapters, small gaps in the seek bar mark them, the current chapter's name sits above the bar, and **Description** starts with a list of chapters you can tap to jump to.

## Gestures

- **Double-tap** the left or right half of the video to go back or forward 10 seconds.
- In full screen, **swipe up or down** on the left third of the video to change the screen brightness, and on the right third to change the volume.
- **Drag the video down** (from anywhere on it) or the bar above it to shrink the player into the small bar at the bottom. Tap that bar to open the player again; its **×** stops playback.

## Full screen

Tap the full-screen button at the bottom right of the video, or turn your iPhone sideways. Turning it upright again returns to the page. On iPad the player stays beside the related videos in landscape instead.

## Comments

**Comments** shows a video's comments. Use the button at the top of the list to switch between **Top comments** and **Newest first**, and **View replies** under a comment to read its thread.
