# Queue and autoplay

The **Queue** tab in the player lists what plays next.


- Add videos with **Play next** or **Enqueue** in any **⋯** menu, or **Play all** / **Enqueue** on a channel or playlist.
- Tap a video in the queue to play it; drag to reorder; swipe left to remove.
- The buttons above the list go to the **previous** or **next** video, **shuffle** the queue, and **repeat** it (all, or one video).

## Autoplay

When the last video in the queue ends, DriftLoom plays a related video next. Turn this off with **Settings → Playback → Autoplay related videos**. Autoplay doesn't run while repeat is on.

## Picking up where you left off

DriftLoom remembers where you stopped in each video and continues from there. Turn this off with **Settings → Playback → Resume playback position**.
