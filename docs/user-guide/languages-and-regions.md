# Language and region

DriftLoom has two separate language choices: the language of the **app** (its buttons and menus), and the language and region of the **YouTube content** you see.

## The app's language

DriftLoom follows your iPhone's language, and you can choose a different one for DriftLoom alone: English, Ukrainian or Portuguese.

1. In DriftLoom, open **Settings** and tap **Language** under *Appearance*. This opens DriftLoom's page in the iPhone's Settings app.
2. Tap **Language** and choose one. DriftLoom restarts in it.

If there is no *Language* row in the Settings app, first add a second language to the iPhone under **General → Language & Region**.

## YouTube's language and region

**Settings → YouTube content** has a **Content language** and a **Content region**. They decide which country's Live, Gaming, Podcasts and trending pages you see, and the language of titles and dates where YouTube offers a choice.

For example, to see Ukrainian live streams, set **Content region** to *Ukraine* and **Content language** to *Ukrainian*. Tap the row, type a few letters in the search field at the top (in English or in the app's language, for example *ukr*) and tap the result. The Live tab reloads straight away; the line at its top shows the region in use. YouTube mixes in international streams, so even then not every stream will be Ukrainian.

## Searching in other languages

You don't need to change any setting to search in another language. DriftLoom recognises the language you type, on your iPhone, and asks YouTube for suggestions and titles in it. See [Search](search.md).
