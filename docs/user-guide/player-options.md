# Speed, quality, subtitles and more

## Playback speed

Tap the speed (for example **1×**) at the top left of the video and choose from 0.25× to 2×.

## Audio only

Tap the **headphones** at the top of the video. DriftLoom switches to an audio-only stream from the same point, which uses much less data, and shows the video's thumbnail. It stays on for the next videos until you tap it again. Audio keeps playing when you lock the iPhone or switch apps.

## Resize

The button next to the headphones changes how the video fills the player: **Fit** shows all of it, **Zoom** fills the frame and crops the edges, and **Fill** stretches it. Your choice is remembered.

## Picture in Picture and AirPlay

Tap **Picture in Picture** to keep watching in a small window over other apps, and **AirPlay** to play on a TV or speaker.

## The ⋯ menu

The three dots at the top right of the player have more options:


- **Quality**: the highest resolution for this video. *Auto* adapts to your connection. To set a default for Wi-Fi and for mobile data, see [Settings](settings.md).
- **Subtitles**: choose a language, *Auto-translate* into another one, or turn them off. Your choice is remembered for the next videos.
- **Playback format**: a specific stream format, when you need one.
- **Stop playback**: close the player.

To save the video for offline viewing, use **Download** under the title. See [Downloads](downloads.md).

To change the default subtitle language and text size, see **Settings → Subtitles**.
