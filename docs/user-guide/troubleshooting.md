# Troubleshooting

## "YouTube didn't provide this video's streams"

YouTube sometimes refuses to play videos for apps that aren't signed in, especially from busy or shared networks, and asks to *confirm you're not a bot*. DriftLoom doesn't get around this. Try again later, or on another network such as mobile data instead of Wi-Fi. Opening the video also stays slower while this happens.

## A video won't download

Live streams can't be downloaded. If a download stops, open **Downloads** and tap **Retry download**; it fetches a fresh link. With **Wi-Fi only** set, downloads wait until you are on Wi-Fi.

## No new-video notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for DriftLoom in the iPhone's Settings app, that *Background App Refresh* is on, and that **Settings → Notifications → New videos from subscriptions** is on. iOS decides when background checks run, and they become more frequent the more you use DriftLoom.

## Some SoundCloud tracks don't play

Tracks that SoundCloud only offers as a preview, or only with copy protection, can't be played in DriftLoom; it says so when you open them.

## The Live tab shows another country

Change **Settings → YouTube content → Content region**. See [Language and region](languages-and-regions.md).

## The app is in the wrong language

See [Language and region](languages-and-regions.md).

## Report a problem

When a video won't play, a page or the comments won't load, or a download fails, tap **Report** next to the error. For anything else, use **Settings → Help → Report a problem** (also in **About**).

- **Open GitHub** opens a new issue on DriftLoom's GitHub page with the error, what was open (the video, page or search), the app version, the iOS version, the device model and the content region. Add what you were doing, then submit it. Nothing is sent before you submit, and the issue is public, so remove anything you'd rather not share.
- **Copy details** copies the same text, for when you don't have a GitHub account; paste it into an email instead.

### After a crash

If DriftLoom quits while you're using it, the next time you open it asks whether to report it, with the same **Report on GitHub** and **Copy details** choices, or **Don't report**. It asks once per crash. If you share analytics with app developers (**Settings → Privacy → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers**), iOS also hands DriftLoom the technical crash details, and long freezes, to include in the report.

Your library, history and subscriptions are never included.
