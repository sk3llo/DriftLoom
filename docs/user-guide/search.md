# Search

Tap **Search** in the tab bar.

## Search YouTube or SoundCloud

Type in the search field and press **Search** on the keyboard. Choose **YouTube** or **SoundCloud** with the switch under the field. **Cancel** empties the field. You can also paste a YouTube or SoundCloud link to open it straight away.

![Suggestions while typing](images/search-suggestions.jpg)

While you type, **suggestions** appear; tap one to search for it. Suggestions follow the language you type in: typing *борщ* suggests Ukrainian searches, *receita* Portuguese ones. You can turn suggestions off in **Settings → YouTube content**.

## Narrow the results

The switch under it shows **All**, **Videos**, **Channels** or **Playlists**. The **filter button** at its right sorts by relevance, upload date, views or rating, and limits the upload date and length.

![Search results with filters](images/search-results.jpg)

For SoundCloud the choices are **Tracks**, **Users**, **Playlists** and **All**.

## Previous searches

With the search field empty, DriftLoom lists your previous searches. Tap one to search again, or swipe it left to remove it. To stop saving searches, or to clear them all, see **Settings → History**.

Search keeps your results while you use other tabs: when you come back, the last search is still there.
